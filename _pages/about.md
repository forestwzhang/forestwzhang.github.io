I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Northwestern University’s McCormick School of Engineering, where I am advised by Dongning Guo. My research interests lie in algorithmic game theory, mechanism design, and the design of decentralized systems involving strategic or adversarial behavior. I am also interested in the interplay between learning algorithms, incentives, and collective decision-making.

I earned an M.S.E. in Computer Science and a B.S.E. in Computer Science with honors and a minor in mathematics from the University of Michigan, where I worked with Ke Wu and Greg Bodwin.
